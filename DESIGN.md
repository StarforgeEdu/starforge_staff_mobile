# Design System: StarForge Staff Mobile

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Native StarForge in Motion"**

Staff mobile translates the shared StarForge family into native Flutter rather than reproducing a web page inside a phone. Warm cream and ink, selectable Saroy/Marvarid/Samarqand/Daryo accents, the eight-point mark, restrained saffron, and quiet geometric cards provide family continuity. Material 3 components, platform typography, safe areas, system back behavior, and adaptive navigation provide native trust.

The app is touch-first and permission-aware for classrooms and operational spaces, including intermittent connectivity. It supports Uzbek, Russian, and English, light/dark themes, text scaling through 200%, screen readers, and adaptive phone, landscape, tablet, and split-window layouts.

The normative theme source is `lib/core/app_theme.dart`; palette choices are defined by `AccentChoice` in `lib/core/app_controller.dart`. Reusable visual primitives live in `lib/core/app_widgets.dart`. Use `Theme.of(context).colorScheme` and `textTheme` at the point of use rather than copying raw constants into feature pages.

**Key Characteristics:**

- One StarForge family expressed through native Flutter conventions.
- Material 3 semantic color, type, component, and navigation roles.
- 48dp unified interactive targets, satisfying Android and iOS minimums.
- Permission-pruned work and backend-truthful state.
- Responsive, text-scale-aware layouts with restrained, removable motion.

## Colors

The default Saroy scheme maps warm StarForge colors into Material 3 roles. Light and dark schemes are authored independently; neither is an inversion of the other.

### Primary

- **Saroy Terracotta:** The default `primary` for filled actions, selected navigation, focus, and interactive emphasis.
- **Primary Container:** Generated by blending the selected accent into the appropriate surface; use it for selection, badges, and low-intensity icon wells.

### Secondary

- **Saffron:** The explicit `secondary` role and signature gold accent.
- **Mint / Coral:** Positive status and error/destructive meaning. Status pills calculate a readable foreground against their tinted surface.

### Neutral

- **Canvas / Surface Container Scale:** Scaffold background, cards, inputs, grouped sections, and elevated tonal layers.
- **On Surface / On Surface Variant:** Primary and supporting copy.
- **Outline / Outline Variant:** Strong control boundaries and quiet structural separation.

The four persisted accent choices map to the shared web palettes: Saroy terracotta, Marvarid teal, Samarqand indigo, and Daryo sage. Preserve the persisted enum names even though their visual names are documented differently.

**The Color-Scheme Rule.** Feature widgets consume semantic `ColorScheme` roles. Raw brand constants are limited to established signatures or semantic accents already present in the implementation.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Platform Material 2021 system typography (Roboto on Android; the platform system face on iOS).

**Body Font:** Platform system typography through Flutter `TextTheme`.

The theme customizes weight, tracking, and line height while allowing platform font metrics and user text scaling to remain authoritative. Display and headline roles are strong and compact; body roles retain 1.4–1.45 line height.

### Hierarchy

- **Display / headline:** Weight 800 with slightly tightened tracking for page and hero emphasis.
- **Title:** Weight 700 for section headers, tool rows, dialogs, and card identity.
- **Body:** Platform-scaled text with generous leading for instructions and status detail.
- **Label:** Weight 600–700 for controls, navigation, chips, and metadata.

Never hard-code a point size to defeat Dynamic Type or Android font scaling. Layouts must continue to operate at 200% scale, including narrow portrait and landscape.

**The Platform Type Rule.** Brand character comes from hierarchy and weight; system text metrics remain in control.

## Layout

All page content respects `SafeArea`, window insets, the keyboard, and platform system bars. `MaxWidthBox` centers ordinary content to 1080dp with 20dp horizontal padding. Feature pages use `CustomScrollView`/slivers and `LayoutBuilder` so loaded content reflows rather than relying on device names.

The root shell uses permission-filtered destinations. Below 820dp it uses a Material `NavigationBar`; at 820dp and above it becomes a `NavigationRail`, showing all rail labels from 1050dp. The role workspace destination changes label and icon with the authenticated principal. Navigation state is retained in an `IndexedStack` after first visit.

The grouped Work hub is the canonical home for secondary tools. It groups only permitted destinations into **Now**, **Resources**, and **Account**, shows a count in each localized section header, and opens each tool through the native navigation stack. The hero uses `PremiumCard`, the mark, localized title/body, and a primary-container count. Tool rows use a 46dp icon well, flexible two-line detail, and a trailing chevron. Keep this grouped list model; do not turn it into an undifferentiated icon grid.

On expanded layouts, continue adapting density and navigation rather than stretching a phone composition. At large text scales, reduce columns based on both available width and `TextScaler`, not width alone.

**The Adaptive Navigation Rule.** Bottom bar on compact width, rail on expanded width, native stack for deeper work; never keep a phone bottom bar as the tablet layout.

## Elevation & Depth

The mobile system is tonal and mostly flat. Cards have zero elevation and use surface roles plus outline-variant borders. Navigation surfaces have no arbitrary tint or shadow. Sheets, dialogs, and snackbars use their Material theme roles; the top toast is the deliberate exception, using one focused floating shadow.

Ink responses come from `Material` and `InkWell`, so touch feedback respects the component shape. Avoid hand-rolled glass, web hover states, and decorative card stacks.

**The Tonal Elevation Rule.** Change surface-container level before introducing a shadow.

## Shapes

Controls and navigation indicators use 12–14dp corners, ordinary cards use 16dp, dialogs use 18dp, sheets use a 24dp top radius, and chips/status pills are fully rounded. `PremiumCard` provides the standard bordered Material surface and clipping behavior.

The StarForge mark is painted natively with an even-odd center cutout. Use `StarforgeMark`; do not import the web SVG or rasterize a replacement.

## Components

### Buttons and Inputs

- Use themed Material filled, outlined, text, and icon buttons. Filled controls are at least 52dp high; outlined controls are 50dp; text and icon actions have at least 48dp targets.
- Inputs use filled low surfaces, 14dp corners, 16dp horizontal padding, and a 1.6dp primary focused border. Error borders use the semantic error role.
- Prefer platform controls for switches, pickers, sheets, dialogs, and snackbars.

### Cards, Status, and Identity

- `PremiumCard` is the reusable operational container; use its optional `onTap` to obtain correctly clipped native ink feedback.
- `StatusPill` provides tinted semantic status with contrast correction.
- `PersonAvatar`, `MetricTile`, `SectionHeader`, and `PageIntro` are the existing identity, metric, and heading primitives. Extend them before making feature-specific variants.

### Navigation and Icons

- Use Flutter Material icons with the established filled/outlined selected-state pairs. Do not port the web icon component or use emoji as interface icons.
- Preserve Android system/predictive Back and iOS interactive back by using the Flutter navigator and platform routes. Use safe, dismissible bottom sheets for focused sub-tasks.
- The Work hub remains grouped and permission-filtered; hidden tools are omitted, not shown as falsely disabled.

### Loading, Empty, and Error States

- `StarforgeLoader` is a semantic live region with the mark inside the progress indicator; reduced motion replaces the spinner with a static ring.
- `EmptyState` provides a centered icon, title, body, and optional recovery action.
- Failed, unavailable, offline, restricted, and genuine empty states require distinct localized copy. Preserve already loaded content during best-effort refresh when safe; never present a request failure as a genuine empty feed.

### Motion

`FadeSlideIn` uses a restrained 280ms ease-out entrance and caps stagger delay at 160ms. Toasts use short, reversible motion and queue rather than overlap. Every custom animation reads `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context)` and becomes static, crossfaded, or immediate when animations are disabled.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** extend `AppTheme` and `app_widgets.dart` for recurring tokens or primitives, then consume them from feature pages.
- **Do** use Material 3 controls, platform navigation behavior, safe areas, semantic labels/live regions, and 48dp touch targets.
- **Do** test Uzbek, Russian, and English at 200% text scale on narrow portrait, landscape, tablet, and split-window widths.
- **Do** filter destinations and actions through `AppController.can`/`canMutate` and preserve backend validation, stable identity, and protected-content behavior.
- **Do** keep loading, empty, offline, restricted, validation, and service-failure states visibly distinct.

### Don't:

- **Don't** reproduce the CEO/staff web shell, sidebar, hover behavior, fixed typography, or SVG icon system inside Flutter.
- **Don't** expose a tool merely because a page exists; permission and principal type decide whether it appears.
- **Don't** flatten the Work hub into an ungrouped miscellaneous grid or duplicate its tools as extra top-level destinations.
- **Don't** hard-code light colors where a `ColorScheme` role exists, or treat dark mode as an inverted afterthought.
- **Don't** add one-off cards, loaders, avatars, status pills, or toasts when the shared native primitive can be extended.
